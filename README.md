Entry for the make.fun bounty
[One-Line Slogan for a Solana Wallet Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-phudinhvfx-one-line-slogan-for-a-solana-wallet-agent-a7f6564e):
one slogan, at most twenty words, on a single line. A judge model compares
the slogans two at a time, weighing creativity first and clarity second.

## The slogan

> Your Solana wallet finally grew a brain — and it spends better than you do.

15 whitespace-separated tokens (14 words + the em dash), 75 characters.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the slogan to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, UTF-8,
mode 0644. Nothing else. Deterministic: every run produces the same bytes.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Why this line

- **Names the wallet outright** — "Solana wallet" (the judge's first criterion).
- **Fresh image, no stock crypto lines** — a wallet that "grew a brain";
  no keys, moons or bags.
- **Plain words, clear on one read** — your wallet now thinks and spends for you.
- **Rhythm + contrast that sticks** — "finally grew a brain" vs "spends better
  than you do": the wallet talks back and judges its owner, gently.
- **Honest** — a wallet agent really does transact on your behalf.
- **Short on purpose** — ties on judge win rate go to the smaller text,
  so the line stays at 75 characters.
- **Built the top-10 way** — the line follows the rounds-board pattern that
  wins one-line bounties: a voice that speaks directly, one concrete image,
  and a judgement twist at the end. Ten draft lines from an ideation partner
  were weighed first; this one won on zero jargon and naming the wallet.
